Acoustic Studio artwork
The user supplied Vintage Guitar Headstock in Warm Vinyl Nook.png as the
visual reference. The isolated photorealistic walnut/brass headstock was
produced with the built-in OpenAI image generation tool and optimized to
WebP with lossless alpha. No competitor artwork is used. The exact prompt
is retained in assets/walnut-headstock-v1.webp.json and the development
asset directory.

Acoustic Studio fonts
Bricolage Grotesque (Studio Display) and DM Sans (Studio Body), reused from
the existing portfolio font files. Both are distributed under the SIL
Open Font License; full notices are included in assets/OFL-*.txt.

Vinyl Room v2 background
assets/vinyl-room-v2.webp is an unchanged copy of the portfolio's existing
dist/assets/atlantic-bunker.webp listening room. Its source metadata is
retained in assets/vinyl-room-v2.webp.json. It is used to keep the tuner
within the portfolio's visual world. No GuitarTuna artwork is included.
